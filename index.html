How the detection actually works (the real tech)

Flash-pattern detection (no ML needed): each video frame is downscaled and split into a grid. Every cell tracks its red-minus-blue value ~10×/sec. A strobing beacon makes that value oscillate at ~5 Hz (red flash → positive spike, blue flash → negative spike). Cells showing 8+ zero-crossings in 3 seconds with both colors bright = beacon. Steady red brake lights never oscillate → no false alarm. Toggle the grid checkbox to watch it work.

Siren detection: the mic feed runs through an FFT (frequency analysis). A wail siren sweeps 700→1500→700 Hz repeatedly — the detector watches the loudest frequency in that band and looks for the up-down-up sweep signature. It hears around corners before the camera sees anything.

Lane choice: vehicles per lane are counted ahead of the ambulance; score = vehicles + 0.4 × (lanes away from ambulance). Lowest score wins — fewest people disturbed. In a real deployment the counts come from an object-detection model; here the sim provides them.
